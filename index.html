The boy I fell for

You once didn't believe me when I told you I'd liked you for a very long time. Maybe because you were looking for one particular moment when it happened.

There wasn't one.

I noticed you gradually. In the way you treated people when there was nothing to gain from it. In the way you helped people without hesitation. In how easily you made the people around you laugh, and in the respect you showed everyone without ever needing to make a point of it.

Somewhere in all those little moments, I realized I had started caring about you far more than I knew how to explain.

The way you care

You have this instinct to show up for people. You go out of your way to help, even when nobody asks you to, and you rarely make a big deal out of it.

I think your kindness was one of the first things that made me see you differently.

The ridiculous one

You have this effortless way of making me laugh, especially when you're being completely ridiculous with me.

I love that side of you. The side that doesn't take itself too seriously, and somehow always knows exactly how to make me smile.

Things I don't say enough

My heart.

Aboudi,

There are so many things I want you to know, and somehow I don't always know how to put them into words when it matters most. So I wanted to leave them here for you.

I love you. More deeply than I think I know how to explain. You have this way of making my days better simply by being part of them. Sometimes nothing special even has to happen. Knowing you're there is enough to make an ordinary day feel a little lighter.

I hope you know how amazing I think you are. I notice the things you probably don't think twice about. The way you help people. The way you give so much of yourself to the people you care about. The way you treat people with kindness and respect, even when nobody is paying attention.

Those are the things I noticed long before you believed me when I told you I'd liked you for a very long time.

And I need to say something else.

I'm sorry.

I'm sorry for the times I snap at you. I'm sorry for the moments when I make things harder than they need to be, or when dealing with me becomes more difficult than it should.

I'm sorry for the times I've hurt you, especially when hurting you was never what I wanted.

I know that loving someone isn't only about the good moments. It's also about learning how to treat each other better, how to listen, how to be patient, and how to grow when we get things wrong.

I don't want you to think that my difficult moments mean I love you any less. They don't. If anything, they make me realize how much I never want my own struggles to become something that hurts the person I love.

I want us to be happy.

I want us to keep growing together, to succeed together, to build a life we're proud of, and one day to look back at everything we've been through and know that we chose each other through all of it.

I want the ordinary things too. The cooking arguments, the stupid jokes, the late-night balcony moments, the days when we're laughing over absolutely nothing, and all the memories we haven't made yet.

And yes, I want the big things too.

A home. A family. A future.

A life with you.

I don't know exactly what every part of our future will look like. But I know that when I imagine the life I want, you're in it.

I love you, habibi.

And I hope you never forget that.
